From Foundation to Finish

Our phased construction methodology provides complete transparency into project milestones, inspections, and quality benchmarks.

01

Consultation & Discovery

We review your vision, architectural plans, site requirements, and provide transparent upfront pricing with zero hidden costs.

02

Architectural Engineering

Finalizing structural blueprints, permits, environmental surveys, and curated material specifications.

03

Foundation & Framing

Groundwork, concrete footings, timber framing, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) rough-ins.

04

Bespoke Interior Trades

Custom cabinetry, luxury tile work, hardwood floor installation, architectural trim, and designer lighting.

05

Final Punch List & Handover

Comprehensive multi-point quality inspection, client walkthrough, and ongoing warranty coverage.
