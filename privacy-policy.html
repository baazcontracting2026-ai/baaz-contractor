Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026 • Baaz Contracting LLC

1. Introduction

At Baaz Contracting LLC ("Baaz Contracting," "we," "our," or "us"), we prioritize client privacy and transparency across all residential design and construction projects. This Privacy Policy applies to our website and interactive architecture portals.

2. Data Collection & Use

We collect personal identifying information (name, phone number, email address, property address, and architectural preferences) strictly when volunteered through our consultation scheduling forms and cost estimators.

This data is used solely to evaluate structural feasibility, prepare itemized proposals, coordinate site inspections, and manage municipal permitting requirements.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with certified project personnel (structural engineers, architects, licensed trade specialists) strictly necessary to execute your contracted scope of work.
