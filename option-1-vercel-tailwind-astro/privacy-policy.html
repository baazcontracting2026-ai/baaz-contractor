Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026 • Baaz Contracting LLC

1. Introduction

At Baaz Contracting LLC ("Baaz Contracting," "we," "our," or "us"), we operate with the highest standards of integrity, craftsmanship, and data protection. This Privacy Policy governs your use of our website (https://baazcontracting.com) and describes how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you request project consultations, architectural reviews, or cost calculations.

2. Information We Collect

When you engage with our digital platform or request construction services, we collect information necessary to prepare accurate project scopes and consultations:

  • Direct Contact Details: Full name, telephone number, email address, and preferred consultation times submitted through our consultation modals or contact forms.
  • Project Parameters: Jobsite street address, property type, scope specifications (e.g. square footage, desired room counts, interior finishes, target budget), and architectural blueprints uploaded or described.
  • Technical Usage Diagnostics: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and referring URLs collected to preserve website security and system uptime.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for legitimate residential contracting business purposes:

  • To schedule complimentary on-site or virtual discovery sessions with our project directors.
  • To calculate accurate construction estimates, material allowances, and municipal permit requirements.
  • To transmit formal proposals, change orders, progress photos, and milestone billing statements.
  • To maintain compliance with municipal building codes, insurance verification, and state contracting laws.

4. No Sale or Commercial Exploitation of Data

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to third-party marketing companies or lead aggregators.

Your information is only shared with essential subcontractors, structural engineers, architectural consultants, or municipal inspectors directly involved in the planning or execution of your contracted project.

5. Cookies & Digital Analytics

Our website utilizes essential session cookies and standard privacy-first analytics to track performance and remember your visual preferences (such as Dark/Light theme mode). You may set your browser to decline cookies, though certain interactive features like our Cost Calculator may experience reduced functionality.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit across our website. Consultation records and client specifications are stored within secure, access-restricted databases. Project documents are retained in accordance with state statutory warranty and contractor lien recordkeeping requirements.

7. Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with applicable privacy regulations (including CCPA and GDPR principles), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate project records, or request deletion of your consultation intake records.
