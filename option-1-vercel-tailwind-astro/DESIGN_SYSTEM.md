# Baaz Contractor: Vercel & Tailwind Design System

## 1. Brand Identity & Creative Direction
**Baaz** represents vision, speed, sharp structural acumen, and elevated perspective. The design system rejects generic traditional blue/yellow contractor templates in favor of an **ultra-modern architectural engineering studio** presence:
- Clean geometry with sharp structural hierarchy
- Razor-thin hairlines (`#E5E7EB` / `#27272A`)
- High-contrast typography featuring **Inter** and **Plus Jakarta Sans**
- Monospaced technical tags via **JetBrains Mono**
- Radiant accents: Electric Cyan (`#00DFD8`) to Azure (`#0070F3`)

---

## 2. Spatial & Layout Grid
- **Container**: Max width 1240px (`max-w-7xl`) centered with responsive padding (`px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`).
- **Base Grid Unit**: 8px spatial increments (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px).
- **Component Radii**:
  - Micro tags / pills: `rounded-full`
  - Input fields & buttons: `rounded-lg` (8px - 12px)
  - Cards & showcase containers: `rounded-2xl` (16px - 20px)

---

## 3. The 7 Service Iconography & Color Tokens
Each service features clear technical categorization:
1. **New Home Construction** -> Structural Framing & Custom Architecture
2. **Additions & Extensions** -> Expansion Engineering & Seamless Integration
3. **Kitchen Remodeling** -> Modern Chef Spaces & Waterfall Stonework
4. **Bathroom Renovations** -> Contemporary Spa Environments & Slate Finishes
5. **Basement Finishing** -> Modern Lounges, Home Theaters & Entertainment Bars
6. **Roofing & Siding** -> Architectural Composite & Clean Vertical Cladding
7. **Flooring & Interior Finishes** -> Wide-Plank Oak Hardwood & Custom Millwork

---

## 4. Accessibility & Contrast (WCAG 2.1 AAA)
- Text contrast ratio exceeds 7.5:1 against light canvas (`#171717` on `#FFFFFF`) and 11.2:1 in dark mode (`#F4F4F5` on `#09090B`).
- All interactive controls have minimum touch targets of 44x44px.
- Visible focus rings with high-visibility cyan outline (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-cyan-500 focus:outline-none`).
